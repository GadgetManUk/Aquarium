Privacy Policy

Effective 26 September 2026. Developer: Paul Hilton.

Summary

Aquarium does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or track any personal data. Aquarium has no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting software development kit, no user accounts, and no network services of its own.

Pointer position

When pointer attraction is turned on, Aquarium reads your pointer's on-screen position in real time so nearby fish can swim toward it. This happens entirely on your Mac. Your pointer position is never stored, logged, or sent anywhere — it exists only for the current moment of animation.

Settings and purchase status

Your preferences — display count, background, fish selection, rendering quality, brightness, and similar settings — are stored locally on your Mac. Your Aquarium Pro entitlement status is also determined and kept locally, derived from Apple's verified purchase records. None of this data leaves your Mac, and it is removed along with the app's data if you delete Aquarium.

Purchases

Aquarium Pro purchases are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. Aquarium does not run its own payment or account system — it only verifies the Apple-signed transaction information StoreKit provides, on your device. Apple's handling of payment and account information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, not this one.

Support and Privacy links

The Support and Privacy Policy links in the About Aquarium window open in your default web browser, not inside the app.

Site hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web request data (such as IP address and browser type) as part of serving these pages, under GitHub's Privacy Statement. This site itself uses no cookies, no analytics, and no scripts.

Children

Aquarium does not collect personal data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as a GitHub Issue.
